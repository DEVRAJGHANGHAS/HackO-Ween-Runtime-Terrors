System Architecture

How FoodResQ AI Works

Four interconnected steps designed to move food safely and swiftly from commercial kitchens to shelter dining tables before expiry.

01

Register Surplus

Hostels, banquet halls, and cafeterias log surplus portions with food category, preparation timestamp, and safe-use deadlines.

02

Match Demand

Deterministic algorithm prioritizes urgent demands, checks recipient shelter intake capacity, and enforces zero over-allocation.

03

Coordinate Delivery

EV couriers receive route orders. Autonomous agent monitors vehicle transit and executes dynamic recovery upon breakdown alerts.

04

Measure Impact

Recipients confirm temperature integrity and portions received. Landfill diversion and CO₂e reduction are logged into auditable reports.
